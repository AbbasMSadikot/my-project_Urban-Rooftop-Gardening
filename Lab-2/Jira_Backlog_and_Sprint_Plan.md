# Lab 2 Jira Backlog and Sprint Plan

This backlog translates the Lab 1 functional requirements into Jira-ready Epics and User Stories. Story-point values are proposed estimates on the Fibonacci scale; a real planning-poker discussion should confirm them with the team.

## Epics and prioritized stories

### Epic 1: Rooftop Plot and Crop Planning

Enable co-op gardeners to register their growing spaces and plan seasonal crops.

| Order | User story | Requirement | Priority | Proposed points |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | As a co-op gardener, I want to create, view, and update my rooftop plot details, so that I can manage the space I use for community gardening. | FR-002 | High | 5 |
| 2 | As a co-op gardener, I want to record a crop and planting date for a registered plot and see an estimated harvest window and expected yield, so that I can plan seasonal planting and harvesting. | FR-001 | High | 8 |

### Epic 2: Harvest Records and Gardening Knowledge

Help gardeners record outcomes and share useful soil-care guidance.

| Order | User story | Requirement | Priority | Proposed points |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 3 | As a co-op gardener, I want to record the crop, plot, harvest date, and actual yield, so that the co-op can track production and compare it with estimates. | FR-003 | High | 3 |
| 6 | As a co-op gardener, I want to publish and browse soil-maintenance tips, so that members can share practices that support healthy rooftop gardens. | FR-004 | Medium | 5 |

### Epic 3: Surplus Produce Exchange

Let the community list, discover, and coordinate surplus produce.

| Order | User story | Requirement | Priority | Proposed points |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 4 | As a co-op gardener, I want to create and update a surplus produce listing with availability, so that other members can find produce offered for trade. | FR-005 | High | 5 |
| 5 | As a community member, I want to browse available surplus listings and request produce, so that I can trade with co-op gardeners. | FR-005 | High | 8 |
| 7 | As a community leader, I want to monitor current surplus listings, so that I can oversee activity on the co-op trade board. | FR-005, NFR-001 | Medium | 3 |

The order column gives the suggested backlog ranking across Epics. Jira priorities are set per story; the Epic descriptions provide the overall theme.

|---|---|---|---:|
