## What the lab asks for

The handout asks for screenshots of the Jira backlog with epics and stories, story point assignments, the Active Sprint board, and a burndown chart, plus a short reflection document.

The lab describes the Scrum board as working like a Kanban board: move issues from To Do to In Progress to Done. It does not ask for a separate Kanban board. “Kanban” is the spelling used in the handout.
